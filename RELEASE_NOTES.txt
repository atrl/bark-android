Bark Android 0.2.2 adds signed APK updates from bark.atrl.me.

Updates can be checked manually or downloaded and installed automatically on unmetered Wi-Fi. Android may require an installation confirmation. Every APK is checked against the published size and SHA-256, the installed package/version, and the existing signing certificate.

Version 0.2.0 does not include the updater. Install this version manually once to receive later app updates.
