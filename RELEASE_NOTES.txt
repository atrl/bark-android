Bark Android 0.4.1 improves basis widget connection recovery.

Temporary connection and server failures now retry automatically up to three
times, with exponential delays starting at one minute. The regular 15-minute
background schedule and manual refresh remain available. Android may delay work.
Valid responses containing old market data do not trigger repeated requests.

Widgets distinguish an initial connection failure from an offline saved snapshot.
Each quote keeps its original source time, explicitly labeled as an as-of time;
sampled prices are not presented as continuously live data. Invalid responses
retain the last verified snapshot and show an update failure separately.
