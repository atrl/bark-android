Bark Android 0.4.0 brings a lighter project dashboard and data widgets.

The native IC card and desktop widget show annualized discount, historical
percentile and a compact close-price trend for the selected IC, IM or IF contract.
Choose a maturity, refresh manually, or tap through to the full project page.
Data timestamps, missing samples and cached/stale snapshots remain visible.

The app and widgets share verified disk snapshots, coalesce requests and use
conditional HTTP updates. Widgets refresh in the background with Android's
15-minute WorkManager schedule; the system may delay execution.
The web viewer has a compact toolbar and reuses the most recent page briefly.
Verified website assets are cached for faster repeat visits and offline use.
