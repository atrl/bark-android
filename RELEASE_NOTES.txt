Bark Android 0.4.2 expands the desktop basis widget.

Standard widgets show all four maturities together, each with its own annualized
basis and same-contract historical closing curve. Small widgets retain two
maturities (front month plus the preferred maturity) and show a 2/4 indicator.
Larger widgets also show historical percentiles and each quote status.

The footer preserves source dates, identifies historical closing curves, and
reports the oldest displayed quote time when maturities differ. Missing history,
missing quotes, offline cache and stale quotes stay explicit. Refresh scheduling
and bounded connection retries are unchanged.
